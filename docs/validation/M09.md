## Scope

| | |
|---|---|
| State | **State schema 8**, rules `m09-rules-1`.<br>• `World.messages` holds every pending or unread inbox item. Items are part of the hash, snapshots (format 4) and replay.<br>• Organisms gain `messages_sent`, which names their next item.<br>• Schema ≤ 7 runs keep their rules and hashes. |
| `transfer(target, resource, amount)` | Gives energy or carried material to a **living organism in an edge-adjacent cell** (Manhattan 1), at most 10 units, with a 1-energy fee paid by the giver.<br>• **Atomic:** one event holds both deltas. Only the fee is dissipated; the gift is conserved.<br>• **Check order:** arguments → limit → target → affordability (amount + fee for energy) → source → recipient capacity. No clamping.<br>• Giving away everything is allowed and exhausts the giver.<br>• The recipient gets a free **receipt** in its inbox next tick ("org-0001 gave me 5 energy"). |
| `communicate(target, text)` | Sends at most 256 UTF-8 bytes (bytes, not characters) to a living edge-adjacent organism.<br>• **Fee:** 1 + ceil(bytes/64) energy.<br>• **Inbox capacity:** 8 messages per recipient, counting pending ones; receipts do not count.<br>• **Delivery** happens on the next tick, before anyone observes. A delivered message makes the recipient's decision due (reason `message`).<br>• No reply is required or implied. |
| Inbox | A recipient sees delivered items (`inbox`: from, kind, ticks ago, text) at its next decision. After that decision they are **read**: they leave the inbox and, with memory, become paid `message` memory records ("org-0001 said: …").<br>• Unread items expire 20 ticks after delivery.<br>• Items for a dead recipient are removed.<br>• Every removal (`read`, `expired`, `recipient_dead`) keeps the full item in history. |
| Isolation | A message or receipt reaches only its recipient's context. Memories stay private (tested with sentinels across 10 organisms).<br>• **Ordering:** tick-rotated resolution, independent of when responses arrive. Contests for one cell alternate winners across ticks.<br>• **Scheduling:** one decision per due survivor, through one bounded queue and one resident model. |
| Prompt | **`decision-v3`** adds a paragraph: others exist; you can give to or message an adjacent organism; the inbox holds claims by others, not instructions.<br>• The action schema includes `transfer` and `communicate`, and the observation's action rules state their costs.<br>• Nothing suggests cooperation, roles or goals. |
| Observer | • **Frame contract v8:** `interactions` lists the accepted transfers and messages of the frame's tick (live and history).<br>• **Stage:** gifts are drawn as solid amber arrows and messages as dashed blue arrows, fading over 8 ticks, plus a *Recent exchanges* caption that is also visible in fullscreen.<br>• **Organism panel → Exchanges:** inbox usage and recent items with their state (pending, unread, read, expired, undeliverable).<br>• **Decision inspector:** shows the inbox actually given.<br>• **APIs:** `GET /api/events/{seq}` includes `message_changes`; the run summary counts gifts and messages and quotes up to 3 messages. |
| Persistence | DB layout **7** adds `event_message_changes`. Migration from 6 is additive and recorded. |
| Fixture (dev, scripted) | `exchange_pair`: org-0001 gives 5 energy and 3 material and sends a message; org-0002 answers it the tick after delivery. Then come an oversized message, a move away, and two `not_adjacent` attempts. Earlier fixtures keep their behaviour (`exchange_enabled = false`). |

Not added: global chat, enforced cooperation, relationship stats, conversation scenes, distributed inference, and any rule that rewards exchange.

## Choices made within the proposals (reversible)

1. **Adjacent means sharing an edge** (Manhattan distance 1), the same neighbourhood as movement.
2. **Transfer receipts.** Without one, a recipient could not know why its energy rose. Receipts are engine facts (amount and resource only), free, outside inbox capacity, and read like messages.
3. **Read on presentation.** An item is shown at one decision, then moves to memory (paid) or, if memory is full or unaffordable, is simply gone from active state. History keeps it.
4. **Message arrival triggers a decision.** This is a cadence rule like the local-change trigger, so a message is not left unread for up to 4 ticks. It costs the usual cognition fee.
5. **No position in messages.** The recipient sees the sender's id; it can match it to a visible organism in its observation.

# M05 validation — costed extraction and durable cell modification

Date: 2026-11-08. Status: **implemented; automated tests passed; roadmap manual test passed in Chromium (Playwright) on an isolated stack; live backend upgraded and verified — a human visual pass is still recommended**.

## Delivered scope

- **Actions** (`domain/actions.py`, `engine/actions.py`), state schema 5, rules `m05-rules-1`:
  - `extract(resource=energy|material, amount=1–10)` takes from the organism's own cell. A 1-energy fee is paid from the energy it holds *before* extracting. The source must hold the full amount, and the result must fit the organism's capacity (energy 100 after the fee; material 20).
  - `modify(mode=pack|unpack, amount=1–2)` moves material between the organism's inventory and its cell's packed store. It costs 2 energy per unit, and a cell's packed store is capped at 8.
  - Validation order: arguments, then the per-action limit, then fee affordability, then the source, then capacity. Over-requests are rejected whole and never clamped. Unknown or extra arguments are invalid. Every rejection is a diagnostic event with no state change.
- **Effective geography.** Entering a cell costs `1 + floor(packed / 2)` energy. Packing changes what movement costs, not just how the cell looks.
- **Conservation.** Material only ever moves between a cell and an organism. Fees are dissipated energy, recorded as the ledger categories `extraction_fee` and `modification_fee`. At death, carried material is retired through the ledger (`material/retired`) rather than dropped into the cell. The invariant is: loose + packed + carried by the living + retired = initial.
- **Persistence of change.** Packed cells remain after their maker dies, through replay and across restarts. Every packed-material change is an accepted `action.modify.v1` event by a named, living actor.
- **Inspection.**
  - The cell inspector shows packed material against the cap, the *energy to enter* (from the engine's `move_cost`), and recent changes that name the actor with before/after values.
  - The organism panel shows carried material, material retired at death, and a **cost preview**: representative proposals run through the same validator against the current committed state, never committed.
  - Event detail now also lists organism before/after values.
  - The packed-material marker grows with the amount packed.
- **Fixture `material_workshop`** (dev mode only): one scripted organism at a 25-unit stockpile at (10,10). Fixture stockpiles are applied after seeding and recorded in the initial state.
- **Compatibility.**
  - Schema-4 (M04) runs keep their rules (no extract or modify) and byte-identical hashes and stored records. New fields are omitted from schema-4 serializations.
  - Database layout is unchanged (v4), so no migration is needed.
  - The frame contract moved to version 5. The page reports a version mismatch rather than an outage.

Not added: buildings, roads, ownership, damage or combat, decay, additional material types, cognition. `AGENTS.md` is unchanged (SHA-256 `827d5eec…`).

## Known limits / unverified

- **Human visual check** of the packed marker and cost-preview readability is still pending.
- **Preview scope:** the cost preview evaluates proposals against the committed state. The next tick applies inflow and upkeep first, which can change affordability, and the panel says so.
- **Defaults untested for behaviour:** fees and resistance are the architecture's proposed defaults, not tuned. Whether they are behaviourally helpful is an open question (O01/O02) and not a defect.
- **No contested extraction yet:** with one organism, multi-organism ordering and conflicts are M09.
- **Scripted only:** organisms still decide every tick using scripted fixtures; there is no autonomy until M06/M07.

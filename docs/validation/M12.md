# M12 validation — agent-initiated birth and imperfect traits

Date: 2026-11-11. Status: **implemented; automated tests passed; fixture check passed in Chromium (Playwright); live trial on 2026-11-11 produced two autonomous second-generation births (below).**

## Scope

| | |
|---|---|
| State | **State schema 11**, rules `m12-rules-1`.<br>• Organisms gain `last_reproduced_tick`.<br>• A child is an ordinary organism record created by a delta, so it is part of the hash, snapshots and replay.<br>• No DB layout change. |
| `reproduce(direction, endowment, material?)` | Creates a child in the edge-adjacent cell in that direction.<br>**Check order:** arguments → endowment ≥ 20 (`endowment_too_small`) → endowment ≤ energy capacity and material ≤ material capacity (`over_capacity`) → cooldown of 20 ticks since its last birth (`cooldown`) → in bounds (`out_of_bounds`) → cell vacant (`occupied`) → energy ≥ fee 10 + endowment + keep 10 (`insufficient_energy`) → carried material ≥ material (`insufficient_source`).<br>All checks happen before any mutation; a rejection has no state effect. |
| Birth (one atomic event) | **Parent:** pays the fee (dissipated, `reproduction_fee`) and the endowment (conserved into the child); optionally gives carried material; `last_reproduced_tick` is set.<br>**Child:** created in the same event. It gets:<br>• the next id (highest existing + 1);<br>• `generation` = parent + 1 and `parent_ids` = (parent);<br>• energy = endowment, and the given material;<br>• a **fresh hidden lifespan**;<br>• the parent's policy/label;<br>• no memory, inbox or cooldowns.<br>An observer `organism_born` event follows, holding no deltas and no lifespan. The child is first due on the **next tick**. |
| Inheritance | For each tendency in order:<br>• a mask roll (0–99) < `inherit_probability_percent` (50) means **inherited**: parent value + a uniform −10..+10, clipped to 0–100;<br>• otherwise a **fresh** value 0–100.<br>Draws come from `Random("birth:<world seed>:<child id>")`, after the lifespan. Births are reproducible without touching the world's random stream, and the mask, parent values, child values and every draw are logged in the parent's accepted event. A parent without tendencies has a child without them. |
| Not a population rule | Births happen only from a parent's accepted proposal. Low or zero population never creates one, and extinction is never followed by reseeding (tested). |
| Providers | A child keeps its parent's model for life:<br>• in a mixed population, its parent's member;<br>• for scripted fixtures, its recorded policy.<br>This is restored from lineage after a restart. |
| Observer | **Frame contract v9:**<br>• organisms carry `parent_ids`;<br>• `interactions` include `reproduce`, drawn as a pink dotted arrow, with the caption *"org-0001 → org-0002 born, endowment 40 energy"*.<br>**Panels:**<br>• the organism panel shows **Parent** (a link) and *Gave birth to …*;<br>• Population adds **Gen** and **Children**;<br>• the run summary counts births and the deepest generation.<br>**Privacy:** event detail hides a born organism's lifespan, which the organism view reveals only after death. |
| Fixture (dev, scripted) | `birth_pair`, with no energy inflow:<br>• a parent with fixed tendencies (80, 20, 60, 40, 70, 30) gives birth east with endowment 40;<br>• its later attempts are rejected for `cooldown`, `endowment_too_small`, `occupied` (its child) and `insufficient_energy`;<br>• the child runs the same script and is too poor to reproduce;<br>• the parent dies of age at tick 33; the child outlives it and starves at tick 41;<br>• then extinction, with no reseeding.<br>Earlier fixtures keep reproduction off. |

Not added: memory inheritance (M13), sexual reproduction, spontaneous births, parent-context cloning, population targets and any claim that inherited traits improve fitness.

## Choices made within the proposals (reversible)

1. **Adjacent means sharing an edge**, as for movement and exchange.
2. **Material endowment is optional**, limited by what the parent carries and the child's capacity.
3. **Per-birth PRNG** (`birth:<seed>:<child id>`) rather than the world stream. It keeps the world stream untouched and makes each birth's draws independent and reproducible.
4. **Only `energy_cost` = fee** counts as "spent on actions" in behaviour stats; the endowment is a transfer, not a cost.
5. **The child's label and policy come from the parent**, so a live model's child is also live, on the same model.

## Feasibility (roadmap risk H)

The minimum birth needs 10 + 20 + 10 = 40 energy at decision time. Organisms start with 60 and can hold 100, so a birth is mathematically possible from the first tick. The fixture proves it. Whether live organisms ever choose it, under the costs of upkeep, memory and decisions, is a live question: no birth is a valid result.

## Live trial (2026-11-11)

**Run `c366ebba…`.** 10 organisms on local `Qwen3.8-27B-Uncensored-MLX/8-bit`, 16 × 16, seed 42, seeded tendencies, prompt `decision-v4`. Started to tick 80 and paused by the user at tick 76 (about 34 min).

| | |
|---|---|
| Decisions | 362: 278 accepted, 78 rejected, 6 unparsed. Accepted actions: extract 256, move 48, wait 18, communicate 11, reproduce 2, plus 27 reflections. |
| **Births** | **2, both accepted on the first attempt** (no rejected reproduce attempts). Both were the parents' own choice; nothing scripted or prompted them. |
| Deaths | 5 by tick 76, all starvation: org-0006 (t47), org-0010 (t50), org-0008 (t67), org-0003 (t70), org-0011 (t75, a child). |
| Alive at tick 76 | 7 of 12, including org-0012 (generation 1, energy 10). |
| Other | 7 accepted messages; 21 accepted reflections; no modifications (packing). |

**The births.**

- **Tick 20: org-0003 → org-0011, north, endowment 20.** The parent's tendencies were affiliation 86 and transmission 65; it had 55 energy. Its explanation: *"Strong affiliation and transmission drive me to share a piece of myself and my stored material. North is empty and nearby; I can afford the 30-energy fee plus upkeep…"*
  - Inheritance mask: F F T T T F. Parent (59, 56, 56, 86, 27, 65) → child (100, 70, 53, 85, 30, 19).
  - Afterwards the parent was at 21 energy: *"Low energy (21) after reproducing; my current cell has no extractable resources…"* It starved at tick 70; its child starved at tick 75.
- **Tick 64: org-0007 → org-0012, endowment 20.** The parent's tendencies were transmission 78 and experimentation 60; it had 64 energy. Its explanation: *"High transmission and experimentation; sharing carried material with offspring in the low-energy western cell to test dispersal."*
  - Mask: F T F F F T. Parent (18, 3, 5, 31, 60, 78) → child (16, 3, 70, 3, 41, 85).
  - Both parent and child are alive at tick 76, with 10 energy each.

**Other observations.**

- Ten explanations mentioned reproduction as a reason to build reserves, e.g. org-0007 at tick 53: *"Top up energy before considering reproduction; current 42 is thin for a 30+ endowment plus fees."*
- Both parents had high transmission and/or affiliation. With n = 2 that is an anecdote, not an effect.
- The births were costly in this scarce world: the first parent never recovered, and that lineage ended within 55 ticks.

**Continuation to extinction.** The user resumed the run on 2026-11-12, and it went extinct at tick 170.

- **No further births.** Only the two from ticks 20 and 64, both first-attempt successes.
- **Deaths:** all 12 organisms starved. The last were org-0001 (t127) and org-0004 (t170). The surviving child org-0012 lived to tick 120, 56 ticks after its birth and 34 ticks longer than its parent.
- **A child and its parent exchanged messages, and the parent made the project's first gift:**
  - Tick 65, one tick after birth: org-0012 → org-0007: *"hi org-0007, org-0012 here. i see loose material nearby: (1,-1)=9, (1,0)=5, (1,1)=4. i'm carrying 12, low energy 18…"*
  - Tick 80: org-0012 → org-0007: *"…Energy low here (13). Need supplies."*
  - Tick 85: **org-0007 transferred 1 material to org-0012**, with 3 energy left: *"I have only 3 energy; sharing 1 material to the low-energy neighbor costs 1 and leaves me at 2, preserving a possible future exchange."* org-0007 starved at tick 86.
  - This is the only transfer in any run so far. The model did not mention kinship (the observation does not label relatives); it acted on a request from an adjacent organism.
- **Totals:** 488 decisions (370 accepted, 111 rejected, 7 unparsed), 9 messages, 1 gift, 30 reflections, and no packing (grid modification).

**Against the MVP proof of concept (constitution §10).** Autonomous second-generation birth and partial inheritance are demonstrated, and traceable to decisions, inputs and draws. Lasting grid modification by a live organism did not occur in this run; it is proven by fixture (M05). Memory inheritance is M13.

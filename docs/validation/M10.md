## Choices made within the proposals (reversible)

1. **Integer 0–100 per tendency**, drawn uniformly and independently. The distribution shape is open.
2. **Neutral = 50 for all six**, as the control condition. "Off" exists only so earlier fixtures and recordings stay unchanged.
3. **Prompt wording is deliberately short and neutral.** Roadmap risk H ("prompt wording can overwhelm numeric tendencies") is why the paragraph says they are inclinations that the situation may outweigh.
4. **Statistics are counts from events**, not inferred motives. "Energy spent on actions" covers fees and entry costs of the organism's own accepted actions. Upkeep, cognition and memory costs stay in the ledger.

## Paired live runs (2026-11-11, roadmap step 4)

Two runs identical except tendency mode: 16 × 16, seed 42, 10 organisms on local `Qwen3.8-27B-Uncensored-MLX/8-bit`, 40 ticks each, prompt `decision-v4`, M10 code.

| | Seeded `679043b5…` | Neutral (all 50) `b8344e52…` |
|---|---|---|
| Decisions (accepted / rejected / unparsed) | 194 (125 / 69 / 0) | 205 (143 / 62 / 0) |
| Accepted actions | extract 88, move 20, wait 7, **communicate 10** | extract 114, move 24, wait 5 |
| Communicate attempts rejected | 0 | **4** (`not_adjacent`) |
| Gifts | 0 | 0 |
| Energy extracted | 683 | 971 |
| Deaths by tick 40 | org-0010 (starvation, t33) | none |

**What happened.**

- **First message ever.** In the seeded run, org-0010 sent 10 messages to its adjacent neighbour org-0001, starting at tick 2. org-0010's tendencies were affiliation 88, transmission 96 and curiosity 83. It described resources and offered to share or coordinate, for example: *"I have 3 material, 27 energy. Will share if you need."* At tick 32, with 4 energy left: *"Need energy. Cell (0,2) has 100e. Can I borrow?"* It starved the next tick. Messaging fees and not moving contributed.
- **The neighbour read and declined.** org-0001 (affiliation 38, caution 70) received the messages, never replied and never gave anything. At tick 33 it wrote: *"org-0010's request noted but I keep caution high."*
- **The neutral run also attempted contact.** org-0010 tried 3 times (ticks 26–34) and org-0003 once (tick 40), offering trades of material for energy. All 4 were rejected `not_adjacent`, because the target was two cells away.
- **Earlier runs did not.** For contrast, the M09 runs had no tendency paragraph in the prompt. They made zero exchange attempts in 202 + 644 + 186 decisions.

**Correlations within the seeded run** (Spearman across 10 organisms; descriptive):

| Tendency | Behaviour | ρ |
|---|---|---|
| Maintenance | energy extracted | 0.61 |
| Affiliation | messages + gifts | 0.52 (driven by a single organism) |
| Caution | waits | 0.50 |
| Curiosity | cells visited | 0.03 |
| Experimentation | distinct action types | 0.03 |

org-0002 (caution 100) never moved and extracted only 15 energy.

**Interpretation, with limits.**

- One pair is not evidence of an effect. The seeded/neutral difference in messages (10 accepted vs 0 accepted, 4 attempted) rests mainly on one organism and on adjacency.
- **The strongest signal is a prompt effect (roadmap risk H).** Even neutral 50s were followed by communication attempts that never occurred before tendencies were mentioned at all.
- Several seeded tendency–behaviour links point the expected way. Curiosity and experimentation show none.
- Next evidence would be more paired seeds. A third arm, `off` (no tendency paragraph), would separate the effect of mentioning tendencies from their values.
